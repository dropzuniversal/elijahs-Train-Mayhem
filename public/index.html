How to play

Drive

  • W / ↑ — Throttle
  • S / ↓ — Brake
  • Space — Emergency brake
  • R — Forward / reverse (stopped only)
  • H — Horn
  • C — Change camera
  • P / Esc — Pause

Work the doors

  • Stop inside the station zone — watch the alignment bar.
  • E opens the doors. Riders walk on by themselves.
  • Board 5 riders to clear the station.
  • E again closes up. The train won't move with doors open.
  • Overshot? Switch to reverse and back it up.

Mayhem

  • Between stations the track breaks for construction ramps.
  • Hit the ramp above 50 mph to clear the gap and score air.
  • The train always lands back on the rails — you cannot derail.
  • Take curves too hot and the wheels throw sparks.
